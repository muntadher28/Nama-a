About this service

Need a Demand Draft? You can submit your request digitally and it will be routed for approval. Once approved, the draft will be printed and made ready for collection at your selected branch.

Please ensure your account has sufficient balance to cover the draft amount and applicable fees. If the required funds are not available, the request will be cancelled.

Processing time:

  • Amount ≤ BD 7,500: Draft will be ready within 1 hour during working hours. You will receive an SMS confirmation once the draft is issued.
  • Amount > BD 7,500: Draft will be ready on the next working day. Our team will contact you once the draft is ready for collection.

About this service

Nama'a is an investment service for corporate clients. Your application is reviewed by the bank, and once accepted your profile is published to a closed group of verified Al Salam Bank investors.

Eligibility conditions:

  • An active Al Salam Bank corporate account in good standing.
  • Classified as an MSME under the Ministry of Industry and Commerce definition.
  • At least 12 months of operating history, with financial statements or a signed financial declaration.

Application steps:

  • Complete the company profile and upload the six required documents.
  • Pay the one-time listing fee from your selected account.
  • The bank verifies your documents and may request additional information.
  • Once accepted, you preview your profile before it goes live to investors.

Fees:

  • Listing fee: BD 50.000 — one-time, charged on submission, non-refundable.
  • No success fee. Al Salam Bank does not take a share of any investment raised.

Expected review duration:

  • Initial review: within 5 working days of submission.
  • Full assessment: up to 15 working days once all documents are complete. If information is missing, the clock restarts when you reply.
